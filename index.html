How to Play Online Blackjack in 4 Steps

  1. Choose your bet. Click or tap the 5, 25 and 100 poker chips until your bet is right — every player starts with 1,000 free virtual chips.
  2. Press Deal. You receive two face-up cards. The dealer receives one face-up card and one face-down (hole) card.

Blackjack Rules Used in This Game

Goal
Beat the dealer's hand without exceeding 21. Going over 21 (a bust) loses immediately.
Card values
Aces count as 1 or 11 (whichever helps your hand), Kings, Queens and Jacks count as 10, and number cards count at face value.
Dealer play
The dealer stands on all 17s — hard and soft. On 16 or less the dealer always draws.
Blackjack payout
A natural blackjack (Ace plus a 10-value card in the first two cards) pays 3:2, that is 1.5× your bet. A normal win pays 1:1.
Double down
On the first two cards you may double your bet, then you receive exactly one more card and must stand.
Deck
A single 52-card deck is reshuffled after every hand, so card counting has no advantage in this version.
Splits
Splits are not available in this simplified version — many free online blackjack demos leave them out.

Blackjack, also known as twenty-one, is a card game where players compete against a dealer. The goal is to have a hand total closer to 21 than the dealer without exceeding 21. Face cards count as 10, aces count as 1 or 11, and going over 21 is called a bust.
